## Direction contract

THESIS: Friends contribute different things over time; SettleUp starts with real bank charges and reveals how the group naturally comes around. Refuse the standard fintech hero beside a polished phone mockup.

OWN-WORLD: Cream field-journal paper, near-black graphite, terracotta route ink, and sage resolved state. Editorial serif headlines meet exact sans-serif money labels and sparse handwritten annotations. Simple comic ink heads replace photoreal avatars.

STORY: Jamie gets pizza, Noor gets drinks and plates, and Eli covers gas and utilities. Their separate transaction paths converge into one friend-group balance. The visitor understands that connected bank data removes manual reconstruction, then chooses to connect a bank or sign in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim header and large serif promise lead into three labeled paths. The map owns the lower two-thirds; exact purchase slips sit beneath simple illustrated heads. A terracotta action remains thumb-reachable.

FORM: Field Note Constellation, second composition “Three Paths Converge,” seed `593481e4`. Scroll traces each route, then zooms out into a gently rotating circle of interconnected phones. Routine product screens inherit the material quietly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Mobile web is primary. Keep money exact and readable, preserve native scrolling, honor reduced motion, and avoid photographic or AI-obvious people. No invented commercial proof.
